What is Scam Shield?

Scam Shield is an AI-based platform that helps job seekers detect fake recruitment calls, suspicious job offers, and fraudulent recruiter behavior. It analyzes job details, phone numbers, company identity signals, and uploaded evidence to estimate scam risk.

How to Use

  1. Create an account or log in securely.
  2. Open the Scam Detection Dashboard.
  3. Enter company details and recruiter call/job description.
  4. Use microphone input to capture spoken details (auto transcription).
  5. Upload evidence: audio, screenshots, and related documents.
  6. Click Start Detection to run AI fraud checks.
  7. Review the risk label, score, explanation, and analytics charts.
